Privacy Policy

Version 1.0  |  Effective October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Triple Maintenance Group, LLC (“TMG,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects through triplemg.com and the forms on it, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting a form, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Contact form. Your name, email address, phone number (optional), your role, and the message you write.

1.2 Discovery call requests. Your name, company, email address, phone number, and the number of locations you manage.

1.3 Provider applications. Business name, contact name, email, phone, taxpayer identification number (EIN) if you provide one, primary and other trades, service states and areas, the number of technicians, and any documents you upload, such as certificates of insurance, Form W-9, and trade licenses. These documents can contain sensitive information, including taxpayer identification numbers.

1.4 Signed Provider Terms. When you sign the Provider Terms, we record your name, title, business name, email, the date and time, the version signed, your IP address, and basic browser information, so we can show who agreed to what and when.

1.5 Automatic information. When you submit a form, we record your IP address to help prevent spam and abuse. Our hosting providers also keep standard server logs. We do not currently use cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics on our website. After you sign the Provider Terms, your browser saves a signature code so your application can fill it in automatically. You can clear it by clearing your browser’s site data.

2. How We Use Information

2.1 Our purposes. We use the information to respond to your messages and requests; review, approve, and manage provider applications; verify licenses and insurance; set up and administer accounts and Service Orders; issue payments and tax reports; keep records of acceptance of our terms; protect our website and prevent fraud and spam; and comply with the law.

2.2 No sale of personal information. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who We Share It With

3.1 Service providers. We use companies that help run our systems, including Microsoft (website back end, database, file storage, and email) and GitHub (website hosting). They may process your information only to provide those services to us.

3.2 Customers and site owners. For providers: we may share your business, license, and insurance information with our customers and the owners and managers of sites as needed to place and perform Work, as described in the Provider Terms.

3.3 Legal and business reasons. We may disclose information to comply with law or legal process, to protect rights, safety, and property, and to a successor in a merger, sale, or reorganization of our business.

4. How Long We Keep It

4.1 Retention. We keep the information we collect, including contact messages, call requests, provider applications, uploaded documents, and signed terms, for seven (7) years, or longer if the law requires, such as for tax records or a legal dispute.

5. Security

5.1 Safeguards. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

6.1 Access, correction, and deletion. You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. We may keep information we are required or permitted by law to keep, and providers who ask for deletion may no longer be able to take part in our network.

6.2 Marketing. We do not send marketing email to people who only use our contact forms. If that changes, we will include an unsubscribe option.

7. Children

7.1 Not for children. Our website is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

8.1 Updates. We may update this Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Your continued use of the website after a change means you accept it.
